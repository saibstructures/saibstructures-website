About Saib Structures

Property Solutions Built Around Your Needs

Saib Structures brings together practical experience in property maintenance, container projects and modular buildings.

We bring over 20 years of experience in container and modular buildings, alongside maintenance work, with an understanding of the practical considerations involved in improving and maintaining properties.

We work with property owners, landlords, property managers, businesses and homeowners on agreed maintenance and improvement projects.

Our approach is straightforward: understand your requirements, agree on the scope of work and focus on delivering a professional result. Where a project requires specialist skills beyond our scope, suitable specialist support can be considered.
